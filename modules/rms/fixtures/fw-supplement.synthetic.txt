Narrative
On June 19, 2024, I, Detective T. Brackenridge #9044, was assigned to follow up on this
case. I contacted the surviving victim, Maren Lindqvist, by phone and arranged to meet
her at the Sample Division office. She told me that she had been standing on the porch
when a dark colored sedan stopped in front of the house and two people got out. She said
the passenger walked up the driveway and began arguing with Benedikt Okafor before she
heard several gunshots. She ran inside the house and called 911.
I canvassed the block for cameras. A resident at 4108 Example Ridge Rd told me that his
doorbell camera faces the street and agreed to provide the footage.

I reviewed the doorbell footage with the resident. At 11:40 p.m. a dark colored four door
sedan can be seen stopping in front of 4100 Example Ridge Rd. Two people exit the vehicle
and walk out of the camera view. About two minutes later both run back to the sedan, which
leaves eastbound at a high rate of speed. The license plate is not readable on the video.
I captured still images from the footage and uploaded them to the case file.
On June 20, 2024, Intel officers located a sedan matching the description parked at an
